Common questions about group benefits

When should we start our renewal?

Sixty to ninety days before your plan renews. That leaves time to review claims and census data, compare carrier options, and roll out changes to employees without a scramble. Tell us your renewal month and we'll reach out at the right time.

Do you work with specific carriers?

We work with the leading carriers and compare options for your group rather than steering you to one. The goal is the plan that fits your budget and your people.

Who helps our employees with enrollment and questions?

We do. Clients get enrollment through Employee Navigator and an account manager who knows the group. Employees and administrators have someone to call.

Is an audit needed?

If you're not certain you're complying with every state and federal requirement that applies to your plan, an audit is the quickest way to know. We'll review what you have and tell you plainly what, if anything, needs attention.

Not sure you're compliant? Let's find out.

Are you sure you are complying with all state and federal regulations? If an audit is needed, we'll work through it with you.
