Common questions about fractional HR

What is fractional HR?

Experienced HR support on a part-time, as-needed basis. You get the expertise of an HR department for the hours you actually need, instead of carrying a full-time salary.

Do we need to replace our existing HR person?

No. We share the HR burden so the people you already have can focus on the most productive parts of the job. Many clients use us alongside an office manager or a single HR generalist.

Which tasks can you take on?

HR compliance, onboarding, background checks, drug testing programs, terminations, employee handbooks and annual training. We tailor the scope to your business, so you get exactly what you need and nothing you don't.

How do you keep problems from happening in the first place?

By looking for them before they surface. Where many large HR firms react after an issue has already happened, we aim to catch missteps early, when they are still cheap to fix.
